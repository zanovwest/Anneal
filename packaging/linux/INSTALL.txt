Anneal @VERSION@ for Linux (x86_64)
===================================

  Anneal.vst3    VST3: Bitwig, Reaper, Ardour, Renoise, ...
  Anneal.lv2     LV2: Ardour, Carla, Qtractor, ...
  Anneal.clap    CLAP: Bitwig, Reaper, ...
  Anneal         standalone app (JACK or ALSA)

Install for your user:  bash install.sh
That copies them to ~/.vst3, ~/.lv2, ~/.clap and ~/.local/bin, the folders hosts scan by
default. Then restart your DAW.

Built on Ubuntu 24.04. It needs the usual desktop libraries: ALSA, FreeType, fontconfig, X11.

Uninstall: delete ~/.vst3/Anneal.vst3, ~/.lv2/Anneal.lv2, ~/.clap/Anneal.clap and
~/.local/bin/Anneal.
